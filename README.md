EVERYTHING ABOUT CUSTOMERS

This is a Customer Management System a full-stack web application designed to help shopkeepers and small business owners efficiently manage
customer records and financial activities in a structured and organized manner.
This application enables users to securely store customer details, track transactions, and monitor payments, providing a centralized dashboard for better business management.


Overview:

The system allows authenticated users to:
	•	Maintain detailed customer records
	•	Add and manage items associated with each customer
	•	Record payments and track outstanding balances
	•	View overall financial summaries
	•	Delete or update customer information when necessary


It is designed with a clean, user-friendly interface to enhance usability and improve workflow efficiency for small-scale businesses.


Key Features:
•Secure Authentication
Session and cookie-based authentication ensures that only authorized users can access the system.
	•	Customer Management
Add, update, and delete customer details efficiently.
	•	Transaction Tracking
Record items sold and payments made by customers.
	•	Financial Summary Dashboard
Automatically calculates:
	•	Total amount
	•	Paid amount
	•	Remaining balance
	•	Overall customer count
	•	Organized Data Structure
All customer activities are maintained in a structured and easily retrievable format.


Technology Stack:

Frontend: React (with modern UI/UX principles)
Backend: Node.js
Database: MongoDB
Authentication: Sessions and Cookies
(MERN STACK)


Purpose:

This application aims to simplify record-keeping for shopkeepers and small business owners by replacing manual bookkeeping with a digital, reliable, and organized solution.

It enhances transparency, improves financial tracking, and reduces errors in managing customer transactions.
